AI Product · Nutrition PROJECT 01

Budget Protein Recipe Advisor

⌥ View source ↗ Live demo
OVERVIEW
  • Given a daily budget, a protein target, and what's in your kitchen, returns protein-rich dish options sorted by cost — and if the target isn't met, the single most budget-efficient item to buy to close the gap.
  • Built as a full Discover → Define → Prioritize → Build → Measure PM lifecycle exercise, not just a weekend script — including a documented scope pivot with defensible reasoning.
  • Deployed live on Streamlit Community Cloud, backed by the Gemini API, with a multi-page app that also includes calorie tracking and step-based calorie-burn estimation.
WHY THIS PROBLEM

I couldn't reliably tell which combination of what I already had — or could affordably buy — hit my protein target most cheaply, every day. Two other ideas were considered and deliberately set aside first: a Blinkit "cook with what I have" feature (Blinkit already ships this via Recipe Rover), and a Rapido "Student Auto" fare complaint (a two-sided incentive problem, hard to solve without internal driver-economics data).

A REAL SCOPE PIVOT

Step/calorie tracking was initially cut because HealthifyMe already does it well. The sharper insight: no existing app connects daily step/calorie tracking to a budget-aware protein engine in one flow. That reasoning was documented, and the features were pulled back into scope.

RISKS & MITIGATIONS
Price / protein estimation risk

AI estimates could be wrong at the actual store. Every figure is explicitly labeled as an estimate, never shown with false confidence.

Fabrication risk

The AI could invent a nutritionally unrealistic dish. Mitigated with explicit system-prompt rules against invention plus manual edge-case testing.

Double-counting risk

Protein logged on two different pages could silently overlap. A disambiguating question forces the user to clarify, instead of the system guessing.

A REAL BUG, FOUND AND FIXED

Early testing showed the AI blending an assumed purchase into "dishes makeable right now." The system prompt was tightened to strictly separate current-ingredient dishes from suggested-purchase items, then re-tested to confirm the fix.
