About

A bit about me

At HCLTech, I work across governance, risk, and security for 42 enterprise banking applications. My role involves coordinating with application owners, technology teams, cybersecurity, and vendors to manage assessments, track remediation, and keep critical controls moving. It has given me a practical view of how large financial technology environments operate — and how important clear requirements, reliable processes, and thoughtful risk decisions are.

I have an MBA in Finance from IFMR Graduate School of Business and a B.Sc. (Hons.) in Agriculture. I’m now bringing that finance and enterprise-technology perspective into AI product building. My projects explore explainable credit-risk assessment, AI-assisted financial decision support, and practical GenAI workflows — with a focus on solving real user problems, communicating model outputs clearly, and keeping human judgment in the loop.
